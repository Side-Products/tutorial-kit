# Code of conduct

Tutorials Kit welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help make its issues,
pull requests, and other project spaces useful and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and code with specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect differing opinions and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and use synthetic data in examples.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone
else's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive content using GitHub's reporting tools. To contact maintainers privately, open an issue asking
for a private conduct-reporting channel without naming people or sharing incident details. Maintainers should
arrange a private channel before requesting those details.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. They should consider the context and impact, explain decisions where appropriate, and protect
the reporter's privacy. This policy applies to project spaces and people representing the project.
